## Full description (max 4000)

```
Orbit turns the area around your front camera into a live, interactive island.

Music, calls, timers, navigation, charging and alerts appear in a small pill centred on your camera. Tap it to expand, swipe it for actions, or just let it keep you informed without opening anything.

IT FINDS YOUR CAMERA
Orbit detects where the front camera is on each screen, including under-display cameras and foldables, and centres itself on it. Foldables keep separate settings for each screen.

WHAT IT SHOWS
• Music: album art, an equaliser, and an expandable player with a draggable seek bar.
• Calls: caller, answer / decline, call timer, mute, audio output and hang up.
• Timers, stopwatches, alarms and navigation, with a live countdown right in the pill.
• Progress for downloads, rides and deliveries, including Android 16 Live Updates.
• Notifications as short peeks, with quick reply where the app supports it.
• Charging animation, battery alerts and a full-charge notice.
• System alerts: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth devices, hotspot, VPN, volume, ringer mode, Do Not Disturb, alarms, flashlight and more.
• Privacy dots for camera, microphone and screen recording.

TWO THINGS AT ONCE
When two things are active, a second small pill splits off. Tap it to swap.

GESTURES
Tap, double tap, long press and swipes in four directions can each run any action: expand, open the app, play / pause / next / previous, home, recents, back, screenshot, lock screen, notification shade, Quick Settings, flashlight, or launch any app. You can even tap the camera itself when the island is idle.

MAKE IT YOURS
Pill size, corner roundness, colours, opacity, outline, glow and position. Match accents to your wallpaper. Choose icons, colours and animations for every alert. Hide it on a schedule, on the lock screen, in landscape, in full-screen apps or in apps you pick.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Orbit has no internet access, no ads and no analytics. Everything it reads stays on your phone.

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
• Notification access: reads notifications and media sessions to show them on the island.
• Accessibility service: used to draw over the status bar and lock screen, to run the gestures you choose, and to notice which app is in front, whether it is full screen and whether the notification shade is open, so the island can hide. Orbit only looks at which windows exist (type, size, app name), never at their content, and does not read or record your screen or what you type.
• Display over other apps: a fallback to draw the island if the accessibility service is off.
• Location (optional): Android only reveals the Wi-Fi name with it. Orbit never reads your location.
• Bluetooth / Phone (optional): show which device connected; answer or hang up a call from the island.

Orbit is an independent project and is not affiliated with Apple Inc.
```
