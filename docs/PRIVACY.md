# Orbit: Camera Island — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Orbit is an Android app that shows a small pill ("island") around your phone's front camera with music, calls, timers, notifications and system alerts.

## The short version

**Orbit does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no internet access.** Everything it reads is used on your phone, to draw the island, and is thrown away.

## What Orbit reads, and why

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications and media sessions** (Notification access) | To show your music, calls, timers, navigation and messages on the island, and to let you press their buttons or reply from it. | Stays in memory on your phone. Not stored, not sent anywhere. |
| **Which windows are on screen** (Accessibility service) | To draw over the status bar and lock screen, run the gestures you choose (home, back, recents, screenshot, lock screen, open the notification shade), and hide the island when an app is full screen, a chosen app is in front, or the notification shade is open. Orbit sees only the type, size and app name of windows, **never what is inside them**. It does not read, record or send your screen, anything you type, or passwords. If you turn on "Replace the system volume panel" it also sees the volume keys. | Stays on your phone. |
| **Wi-Fi name** (Location permission, optional) | Android only reveals the name of the Wi-Fi network you connect to if an app holds the location permission. Orbit uses it only to show "Connected to <name>". **Orbit never reads your location.** | Stays on your phone. |
| **Bluetooth device name and battery level** (Nearby devices permission, optional) | To show which headphones or device connected. | Stays on your phone. |
| **Phone permission** (optional) | To answer or hang up a call from the island when the call's notification has no button. | Stays on your phone. |
| **Camera / microphone / screen-recording activity** | Android tells Orbit when another app starts using them, so it can show a green, orange or red dot. Orbit never accesses the camera, the microphone or the screen content itself. | Stays on your phone. |

## Your settings

Your choices (colours, gestures, which apps to hide in, and so on) are saved in Orbit's private storage on your phone. If you uninstall Orbit they are deleted. Android's backup may include them if you have device backup turned on.

## No third parties

Orbit contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party code. It does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

## Children

Orbit is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
